## Phases of Natural Theory

- Human Reason is common among men and along with this, the common precepts of right law and equity. Plato, Aristotle, Socrates
- Scholastic Phase taught that natural law is man’s participation with eternal law
- Enlightenment and Modern phase used natural law as a basis for natural rights and duties
- International law phase, a common law for all nations of men
## Classical Theory

### Plato

- Founder of Platonist School of Thought
- Philosophy: Republic and laws
- Regime, ruled by a benevolent dictator, an educated philosopher king
- **Natural law is not the law of the common man but the law of the ideal man**
- Punishment is not necessary provided through education and socialization the people have been predisposed to observe what the state contemplates as right via rational persuasion
### Aristotle

- Student of Plato
- Founder of Lyceum
- Father of Biology
- Human beings have a rational nature that must be followed as a matter of law. Although there are particular or conventional laws suited to each culture and times, there is a common law, a natural law, a general justice by which men can objectively judge whether the law can serve as being human?
- Reason makes us perceive what is excessive, pursue a balanced life, and to seek what is appropriate, fair, just, and right, other words the **Golden Mean of Living.**
- **Happy man is an ideal man with disposition virtue, character, moderation**
- Man is a social animal that needs to live in the community.
- Family, neighborhood, city state.
- Monarchy, aristocracy, tyranny, oligarchy, radical democracy, polity.
### Tyranny

- Unlimited and absolute power vested in one person
### St. Thomas Aquinas

- The universe is governed by divine reason through an Eternal Law, or natural law.
- By nature of man, he will know what he out to be and out to do.
- Difference, divine revelation.
- Divine law is needed for him to realize his supernatural ends.
- Question 94, Summa, with 5 inclinations:
  - Primary inclination to do good and avoid evil
  - Good meaning, sustain ourselves to self-preservation
  - Perpetuate ourselves, sexual instincts, procreation, while lust is natural, so are shame and self-restraint
  - Live in community, men families, societies
  - Use reason and will, know truth, make own decisions
## Natural Rights

- Appertain to man in right of his existence
- Fundamental rights endowed b god upon human beings
- Rights of individual for his own comfort and happiness, which are not injurious to the natural rights of others.
